How it works

Up and studying in minutes

Set up your profile

Add your university, department and year so Steth shows your courses and classmates.

Add your subjects

Tell Steth what you're studying and it builds a plan for your day.

Learn, quiz, repeat

Work through the plan, earn points, grow your streak and move up the ranks.

Ready to climb the ranks?

Steth is an Android app, and iPhone users can use the web app. Pick yours and make today's study session count.

Android

Install the Steth app on your Android phone (APK, 64 MB).

Download forAndroid
  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file.
  2. If asked, allow Install from unknown sources.
  3. Tap Install.

iPhone

Use the Steth web app in Safari, no App Store needed.

Open theiPhone web app
  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
